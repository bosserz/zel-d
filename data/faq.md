# Zel-D FAQ & Policies (Demo)

## Shipping
- Probiotic drinks and clean meals require cold-chain delivery.
- Typical delivery time: 1–3 business days (varies by location).
- If cold-chain delivery is not available to a location, those items may be unavailable.

## Storage
- Probiotic drinks: keep refrigerated (2–8°C).
- Ready-to-eat clean food: keep refrigerated (2–8°C) and consume within the stated shelf-life.

## Refund / Return
- If the order arrives damaged or temperature-compromised, contact support within 24 hours with photos.
- For non-perishable items, returns may be accepted if unopened within 7 days of delivery.
- Perishable items are generally not returnable unless there is a quality issue on arrival.

## Allergens
- Always check product allergens.
- If you have severe allergies, contact support before ordering.
